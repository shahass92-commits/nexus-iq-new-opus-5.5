11 · The ETS operating layer

Every shift, every seat, every kilometre — run from one layer.

Employee transportation is a daily logistics problem with a hard deadline: the shift starts whether the cab arrives or not. Nexus iQ turns rosters, fleet, routes and billing into one operating layer, so the corridor runs on evidence instead of phone calls.

E1

Smart employee transportation orchestration

Shift rosters arrive; routes leave. Nexus iQ clusters employees by address and login window, assigns the right vehicle class, sequences pickups against the clock and pushes the manifest to the driving partner — before the shift planner has finished their coffee.

  • Route out clustered pickups, sequenced to the minute
  • Seat-fit sedan, MUV or EV picked per cluster, not per habit
